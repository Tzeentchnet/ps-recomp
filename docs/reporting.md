# Report a problem

Open an issue on the project's
[GitHub page](https://github.com/plaught-armor/ps-recomp/issues). Please
include:

- Your system (Linux, Windows or macOS) and its CPU type.
- The ps-recomp commit you built from (`git rev-parse HEAD`).
- The command you ran, with any `PS_` settings.
- What you expected, and what happened instead.
- The text `play` printed in the terminal.
- If you can, a [compatibility run](compat.md) of the game: its row from
  `compat.tsv` and its `compat.log`.

Playing in the browser? Press **Save a report** on the play page after the
problem. It opens the issue with the run's details filled in; see
[Play in your browser](browser.md#reports-and-game-data).

**Do not attach disc images or game files.**
